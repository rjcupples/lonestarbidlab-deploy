What My Days Look Like

I spend my days estimating wet utility construction—water, sewer, storm drainage, the stuff that goes in the ground before anything else does. That means plans, takeoffs, quotes, risk assessments, and figuring out how to price work that hasn't been built yet.

When I'm not buried in bid tabs, I'm usually frustrated with some piece of estimating software—so I build tools to fix it. Automation for takeoffs, AI agents that help with repetitive tasks, whatever needs building.

A typical day involves:

  • Reading plans and doing takeoffs
  • Chasing down material quotes
  • Pricing water, sewer, storm
  • Excavation and paving restoration calcs
  • Bid strategy and risk calls
  • Breaking/fixing automation scripts
